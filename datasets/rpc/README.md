# RPC Dataset (Retail Product Checkout & SKU Recognition)
Location: `datasets/rpc/`

## Purpose:
- Retail product checkout recognition across 200 categories & 1,850 SKUs
- Touch-to-cart conversion funnel modeling
- Product identification under varying shelf lighting and angles

## Directory Structure:
- `single_sku/` - Single product images on checkout counters
- `checkout_clutter/` - Multi-product checkout cart images
- `annotations/` - Category and SKU catalog mappings
