# Retail Store Traffic Dataset (Consumer Movement Analytics)
Location: `datasets/retail_store/`

## Purpose:
- Customer trajectory tracking, walking paths, and entrance movement analytics
- Zone dwell time calculations (Beverages, Snacks, Cosmetics, Checkout)
- Heatmap density mapping and queue congestion detection

## Directory Structure:
- `videos/` - Store CCTV surveillance footage clips (MP4, AVI, MOV)
- `annotations/` - Trajectory tracking logs and ground truth bounding boxes
